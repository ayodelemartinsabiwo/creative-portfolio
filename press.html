TOP TEA / OCCASION-LED PRINT

The warmth that shapes us.

Turn the heat of a cup of tea into a metaphor for a father’s influence.

Steam becomes a smiling portrait, connecting the headline about a father’s “heat” with the product ritual. The red cup creates a clear anchor against warm wood, while the pack range completes the brand story.

The design approach
Steam-portrait compositing · visual metaphor · copy–image relationship
